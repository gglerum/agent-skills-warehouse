# Decision questionnaire

Explain who should answer and which decision their answers inform. Give concrete scenarios and mutually distinct options in plain language; allow observations outside the suggested choices.

Separate established facts, hypotheses and the recipient's judgments. Ask only questions the repository cannot already answer. Preserve the actual answers and their context; do not turn a prototype reaction into a universal claim. Record the disposition of unanswered or conflicting questions without inventing agreement. Use [the template](questionnaire.md) when it fits.
